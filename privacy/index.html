Jojo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Jojo helps parents keep their family's health records — vaccinations, prescriptions and lab results — in one place on their iPhone. This policy explains what Jojo does with that information. In short: there's no account, and your family's health records stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.

Who we are

Jojo is made by Ahmed Musallam, 6th of October City, Giza, Egypt. Contact: ahmed.hamdy.musallam@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Family members: names or nicknames, dates of birth, and the avatar you choose.
  • Health records: vaccinations, medicines and prescriptions, lab results, and the photos and PDFs you add.
  • Reading documents: when you add a photo or PDF, Jojo reads it on your iPhone (Apple's on-device text recognition). Nothing is sent anywhere to read it.

This information is stored on your iPhone and protected by iOS data protection while your phone is locked. It's included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup (Apple's service, set up by you in iOS), so it moves to a new iPhone you set up from that backup. Jojo itself never receives it.

Accounts

Jojo has no account and no sign-in. We never receive your email, phone number or name, and we have no server that stores your records.

How we learn what to improve

Only if you agree: Jojo asks once, and you can change your answer anytime in Settings › Privacy and data › Share usage data. Until you agree, nothing is sent. If you agree, Jojo sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (hosted in the EU): which screens you open, actions such as "a record was added" with its type (vaccine, medicine or lab report) and how (camera, photo or file, or typed in), and app errors. They are tied to a random ID created by the app, not to you.

They never include health information: no medicine, vaccine or lab names or values, no documents or photos, and no one's name. There is no screen recording.

Each event also says whether the country you chose in Jojo is Egypt, another country, or not chosen; which other country you picked stays on your iPhone. Jojo asks PostHog not to work out your location from your IP address.

Provider: PostHog (product analytics, EU — Frankfurt).

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't use advertising tools. The only analytics are the anonymous usage events described above.
  • We don't share your family's information with anyone.
  • Jojo doesn't give medical advice. Vaccine schedules come from each country's published national schedule (today Egypt's Ministry of Health and VACSERA, shown only when your country is Egypt), and lab ranges and flags are shown as printed by the lab.

Your choices

  • Turn usage data on or off anytime in Settings › Privacy and data › Share usage data.
  • Delete everything on your iPhone in Settings › Privacy and data › Delete all data. Because we never receive your records, there's nothing of yours on our side to delete.

Children

Jojo is for parents and guardians. Children don't use Jojo themselves; parents add their children's information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
